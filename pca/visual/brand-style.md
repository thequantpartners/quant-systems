# Brand Style

## Direction

Editorial operator console: calm, high-contrast, direct, and practical rather than a generic AI
SaaS page. The landing should feel like a serious system for reducing operational waste.

## Color system

- Warm paper background and dark ink for readability.
- Strong Telegram blue (`#155eef`) for the Telegram horizontal, primary emphasis, and key links.
- Dark Telegram blue (`#0b3fb8`) for smaller interactive accents.
- Pale blue for supporting states.
- Avoid green as the dominant accent; it belonged to the previous WhatsApp-oriented visual direction.

## Copy behavior

Lead with a scene the operator recognizes, name the cost in time/capacity/conversion, show the
operational change, and ask for a diagnostic. Avoid guarantees, artificial urgency, fake anchors,
financial promises, signals, personalized investment advice, gambling language, and unverified
performance claims.

## Layout and accessibility

Keep mobile-first, visible focus states, readable contrast, and zero horizontal overflow. Use
asymmetric editorial sections and problem-led rows instead of generic card grids. The first viewport
must identify the Telegram horizontal and the measurable operational outcome quickly.

# Product Context

Quant Systems validates and later implements operational agents for Telegram communities. The
initial market grouping is trading, forex, and financial-education communities in Peru and
Spanish-speaking markets. The product is not a generic chatbot and is not sold as an n8n product.

The commercial promise is to reduce measurable manual work or improve a measurable conversion
process using Telegram as the interface and the customer's existing tools as the system of record.
Candidate workflows include lead qualification/follow-up, repetitive support, and class/cohort
coordination.

The landing must qualify a real bottleneck, its frequency, its consequence, and a before/after
metric such as manual hours, qualified conversations, attendance, follow-up, or attributed
meetings/enrollments. If the prospect cannot identify a measurable impact, the solution is removed.

Google Ads is the first paid acquisition channel. Telegram Ads is not part of the launch plan because
funding requires TON/wallet setup. Telegram remains the operational horizontal and eventual bot
destination.

The validation landing and lead alert path are operational in production. The lead form posts to
FastAPI, persists before sending a private Telegram alert, and sends the user to the active bot
after success. Telegram can support rich alert formatting and interactive buttons, but that is a
future workflow enhancement rather than part of the current acquisition validation.

Trading/forex content must remain educational and operational. Do not offer signals, personalized
investment recommendations, guaranteed returns, get-rich-quick claims, or betting/gambling offers.
